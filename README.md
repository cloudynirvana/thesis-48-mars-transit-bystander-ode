# Thesis #48: Radiation-Induced Bystander Effects during Mars Transit

This repository contains **Thesis #48** of the independent computational research series by Kelechi Emeka Ogbonna.

## Overview
This manuscript formulates a stochastic ordinary differential equation (ODE) to model the radiation-induced bystander effect (RIBE) under the specific stochastic forcing of Solar Particle Events (SPEs) anticipated during a deep-space Mars transit. It addresses critical gaps in the direct-hit models currently used for space dosimetry and assesses the structural identifiability of bystander kinetics from aggregate DNA damage metrics.
